Privacy Policy

App: Client Khata

Developer: VPSoftworks

Effective date: November 1, 2026

VPSoftworks ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how the Client Khata mobile application handles information when you use the app.

1. Information Stored by the App

Client Khata is a customer ledger application. Depending on how you use it, you may enter and store information such as:

  • Customer names and related customer details.
  • Transaction amounts and transaction types.
  • Transaction dates and related ledger records.
  • Other information you choose to enter into the app.

This information is stored locally on your device in the app's database. The current version does not provide cloud synchronization or Google account login.

2. Data Sharing and Advertising

Client Khata displays banner advertisements using Google AdMob. Google and its advertising partners may collect or process information such as device identifiers, IP address, advertising data, diagnostics and ad interactions to provide, measure and improve advertising services.

Advertising data handling is subject to Google's policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from apps that use its services

We do not sell your customer ledger information. We do not intentionally share customer names or transaction records with advertisers.

3. Data Export

The app may allow you to export customer or transaction information into supported file formats. Exported files are under your control. Please store and share these files carefully, as they may contain financial information.

4. Data Storage and Security

Customer and transaction records are stored locally on your device. We recommend keeping your device secure and using available device-lock features.

We cannot guarantee the security of information stored on a device that is lost, compromised or accessed by an unauthorized person.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

Your locally stored ledger information remains on your device until you delete it using the app's available features, clear the app's data, or uninstall the app.

Exported files may remain in other locations on your device or in storage services where you saved them. You are responsible for deleting those copies.

For privacy-related questions or assistance, contact us using the email address below.

6. Children's Privacy

Client Khata is a business and customer ledger application and is not designed specifically for children. We do not knowingly request personal information directly from children.

7. Third-Party Services

The app uses Google AdMob for advertising. Third-party services operate under their own privacy policies and terms. We encourage users to review those policies.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when app features or applicable requirements change. Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised effective date.
